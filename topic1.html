Topic 1: Introduction & Concepts of World Wide Web (WWW)

Core Concept: The World Wide Web (WWW), often called the Web, is a system of interconnected web pages and information that you can access using the Internet. It was created to help people share and find information easily, using links that connect different pages together.

  • Global Interaction: Allows users to browse websites, watch videos, shop online, and connect with others worldwide.
  • The W3 Pool: All public websites accessible through the internet on any local device are collectively known as W3.
  • Hyperlink Navigation: Users discover further information by navigating interlinked pages and documents.
  • Rich Formats: Information is represented in diverse text, picture, audio, or video formats.
